OfflineMesh

Chat with the people around you when there is no signal: phone to phone, no internet, no towers, no servers.

The problem

Phones go silent exactly when people need them most.

At protests, after disasters and at packed festivals, towers go down or jam. The person you need may be 200 m away and still out of reach.

Safe by design

  • Signed messages. No one can fake or alter a post.
  • Private DMs. End-to-end encrypted. Relays cannot read them.
  • Proof of work. Every message costs a little computing, so spam is expensive.
  • Flood bans. Break the rate limit and your key is banned.
  • Padding. Hides how long a message is.

How it works

  1. Every phone links to the phones near it and passes messages on.
  2. Posts reach everyone within 6 hops; DMs take one learned path.
  3. If a relay drops a DM, the sender sees no receipt and reroutes.
  4. A phone that comes back in range catches up on what it missed.
